Located in downtown Bastrop, Texas, Goats Only Recording Studio is a boutique audio production and creative services studio specializing in mixing, mastering, sound design, music production, and education.

The studio works with artists, producers, and creative professionals who are looking for a more personal, collaborative approach to their projects. Services include mixing, mastering, sound design, podcast editing, music beds, stingers, sweepers, voiceovers, and custom audio solutions.

Goats Only also offers one-on-one music production and career consulting, along with personalized tutorials and hands-on instruction designed to help musicians, producers, and DJs develop both their creative skills and their professional careers.

Recording and production sessions are available by consultation and appointment. Every project begins with a conversation about your goals, your project, and how Goats Only can best help bring it to life.
